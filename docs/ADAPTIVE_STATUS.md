The frozen model's measured peak process RSS is about **3.25 GiB** on GitHub Actions with two inference threads, before unrelated host processes. This is near the user's 4 GiB target and is **not** a guarantee that the same complete setup runs comfortably on a 4 GiB machine. The pilot has only two short tasks and its nominal private fixtures have been repeatedly viewed by researchers; **do not call it an independent generalization score**. The pilot is closed to further tuning on those examples.

Z.ai's independent Session 034 used the **base** Qwen checkpoint and produced staged browser apps with transparent teacher/harness attribution. Its [measured Tier V report](https://github.com/7clan/ai-wieght-optimiization/blob/exp/zai-visual-intelligence/docs/ZAI_VISUAL_INTELLIGENCE.md) is a separate research outcome; do not import its task scores as our own or mutate its branch.
